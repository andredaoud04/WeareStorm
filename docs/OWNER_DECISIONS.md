# WeAreStorm — Owner decisions

Decided by the owner (2026-10-13):
- **Art style:** clean cartoon everywhere (no pixel art, no RPG frames).
- **Design:** We Are Warriors-style core loop first; keep the six Ages; cards/skills/dungeons after the core feels great.
- **References** (Drive concept boards) are inspiration only; creativity encouraged.

Open — the build proceeds with the recommended default until the owner says otherwise:

| # | Decision | Recommended default |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Flags on bases | Small faction pennant on a free-standing pole behind each base (ART_BIBLE §5.9); no cloth, banners or flags on the architecture in any Age |
| 2 | Battle 1–5 tutorial mascot "Nimbus" (a small storm cloud) | Keep — short one-line bubbles, skippable |
| 3 | Storm Rising overtime (both sides hit harder after 100 s; bases take double damage from 120 s) | Keep — prevents stalemates, on-brand; reached in 25% of regular battles and 91% of Warlord battles |
| 4 | Evolution cost | Free after the Warlord; paid early Evolve available (180 / 420 / 820 / 1,450 / 2,500 Gold, GDD §3.5). Camp upgrades reset each Age, slot unlocks persist |
| 5 | Game title on store/home screen | "We Are Storm" (repo name WeareStorm) |
| 6 | Per-role troop training tracks (Melee/Ranged/Tank Lv 1–10) — reverses the Stormglass "no campaign unit leveling" rule | Default: keep for v1. Alternative: Food + Base only |
| 7 | Distracted players (2–6 s reactions) win almost no new regular stage on the first try (0–10% from stage 4 on, 20–70% on Warlords) and finish through the lose-forward assist in about 5 attempts per stage (BALANCE §4.9). 3-wave assaults were tried and rejected | Default: no change; decide after the G1 playtest. Candidate if playtests agree: a modest gate guard for the player's base |
